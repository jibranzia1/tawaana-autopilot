```text
Save this one for later.

1. Preheat 1 to 2 minutes on low-to-medium flame.
2. Wooden or silicone spatula only.
3. Let it cool, then wash with a soft sponge.
4. A few drops of oil, no aerosol spray.
5. Dry fully and hang it by the loop.

Simple habits keep your Tawaana cooking for years. tawaana.com
```
